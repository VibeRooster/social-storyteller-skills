## Formula
1. **Hook** (~3–5s, usually to camera): the problem or a surprising claim, in one line.
2. **Explain**: what the thing is, who made it, its status. Name the original creator.
3. **Illustrate**: show it working on screen, one action per line.
4. **Teach**: the takeaway: how to use it, how to read the result, why it matters.
5. **Close**: the CTA (see `cta-library`).

## Beat table
| Time | Section | Line | On-screen | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Source values: **creator** (paraphrase of the creator's own commentary), **page text** (read from the screen), **editor framing** (makes no factual claim). Mark gaps only the creator can fill as `[CREATOR: …]`.

## Timing
- ~2.6 spoken words per second, plus a breath per line. Start times are cumulative.
- Deep-dive video: ~50–55s (~130–140 words). Overview: ~55–65s.

## Steps
1. Pick the funnel stage and CTA first.
2. Write 5 hooks (`hook-writing`), pick one, keep a spare.
3. Fill the beat table; source every factual line (`proof-points`).
4. Read it aloud against the clock; trim to length.
5. List open questions and `[CREATOR]` gaps for the human.

Examples and the H.I.D.E. story variant: `references/`.
