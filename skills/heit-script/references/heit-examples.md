# HEIT examples

**Demo example**
- HOOK: If the AI keeps missing, it might be your prompt, not the model.
- EXPLAIN: This is the Prompt Playbook, an interactive take on [creator]'s playbook.
- ILLUSTRATE: A weak prompt, then a strong one. Name the language. Paste the exact error. Pick your job, hit assemble.
- TEACH: So stop guessing. Be specific, and let it build the prompt with you.
- CLOSE: Save this for later. The link is in my bio.

**Question-hook example**
- HOOK: Why does your AI judge give almost everything a 4.8?
- TEACH punch: The judge isn't broken. Your rubric is.
