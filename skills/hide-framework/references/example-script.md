# H.I.D.E. sample script with shots

This is the source template's own sample.

| Beat | Shot | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Shot 1 (wide) | Feeling overwhelmed when talking about money? |
| Intrigue | Shot 2 (mid long) | Last week I was working with someone who was trying to set the hourly rate for their business and looking at their competitors and "the going rate." |
| Deliver value | Shot 3 (mid close-up) | But a one-hour session with them could have unlocked a million pounds' worth of business. So how much was that session really worth? |
| Exit with action | Shot 1 (wide) | So, if you're a [Business Coach] trying to [build your business], follow me for more content like this. |

The Exit returns to Shot 1, the same framing as the Hook.
