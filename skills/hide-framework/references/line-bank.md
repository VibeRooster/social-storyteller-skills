# H.I.D.E. line bank

Choose one line from each section and fill in the blanks.

## 1. Hook
Grabs attention. It can be visual, audio, text, or all of the above. Have your ideal client in mind.
1. If you want to ______
2. This is why your ______ isn't working
3. Feeling overwhelmed with ______? Try this.

## 2. Intrigue
A one- or two-sentence story. It delays the value and increases watch time.
1. I was working with a client that ______
2. I always thought that ______
3. Last week I was ______ and that's when it hit me.
4. People often think that you should ______

## 3. Deliver value
The point of your video: the information, advice or wisdom you want to share.
1. Start by ______
2. But ______
3. Now I know ______

## 4. Exit with action
Get your viewer to do something (like a post, follow you, buy something, DM you) so they engage with your content and remember the value you gave them.
1. So if you're a (ideal client) trying to (do ideal task), then follow me for more tips like this.
2. Do you agree?
3. Try this tip and let me know how you get on.
4. Tap follow for more tips like this.
