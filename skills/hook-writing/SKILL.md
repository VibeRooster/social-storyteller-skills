# Hook writing

1. **Write 5 hooks.** Spend ~80% of effort on the hook and packaging. Pick one; keep a spare for A/B.
2. **Keep it to one breath**: 6–8 words on screen.
3. **Lead with the best part**, and make sure the body delivers on the hook.

Patterns: `references/hook-patterns.md`.

## A/B skip-rate test
- Build two ~3–4s hooks on an identical body: A = evidence-first screen footage (stat/result on screen); B = direct-to-camera usefulness line.
- Post or test both; compare 3-second hold and skip rate.
- Keep both as versions. Never overwrite either.
