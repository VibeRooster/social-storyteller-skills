# Hook patterns
- **Promise / transformation**: "3,100 words to 1 interactive quiz." Trim long ones ("Skip 3,100 words, take the quiz.").
- **Counterintuitive / evidence-first**: open with the surprising number or on-screen proof. Stats must be sourced.
- **Problem / question** (video): "If the AI keeps missing, it might be your prompt, not the model." Fine on camera; avoid question openers in written long-form if your voice guide forbids them.
- **Emotional title card**: big display type over the first 3s.
