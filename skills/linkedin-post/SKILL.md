# LinkedIn post

## Structure
1. Hook line: a flat axiom or a specific number.
2. White space.
3. The insight or story, best part first.
4. Outcome, then the steps or process.
5. One proof point or media item that proves it.
6. A closing punch line (no forced ending; follow your voice guide on CTAs).

## Pipeline
Mine a real insight → pick brand and funnel stage → write 5 hooks → draft in the author's voice (`voice`) → attach proof media → fact-check (`proof-points`) → return for human review. **The human posts.**
