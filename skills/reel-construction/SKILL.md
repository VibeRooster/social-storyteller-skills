# Reel construction

## Series plan
- A weekly **overview** (sells *why* the selection matters) plus numbered **deep dives** (n/5) that teach *how to use* each pick and how to read its results. Tag each in a top corner (e.g. `SERIES · WEEK N · x/5`).
- Optional teaser/montage intro under 10s.

## Funnel (pick before scripting; it sets the CTA)
- **TOF** awareness: broad hooks, basics, emotion.
- **MOF** consideration: overviews, deep dives, proof.
- **BOF** decision: a specific offer or next step.

## Shot grammar
- Change the beat ~every 3s: cut angle, punch in, go to screen, or cutout.
- Background-removed speaker cutouts as small picture-in-picture over screen footage; alternate angles.
- Lip sync: where the face is readable, use in-sync takes only; list sync windows in the timing sheet.
- Intercut angles at sentence boundaries; audio-sync only when takes match word for word.
- Screen captures: 1080x1920 browser recordings, slow scrolls, no clipping zooms, start each page fresh.
- Graphic pop-ins at the peak of a gesture, held ~2s; soft dark backing on busy backgrounds.
- Evidence graphics only for concrete, sourced claims.

## Cards
- Titles/stat cards in the top band, big white type in the upper third. Keep the bottom band and right ~15% clear for platform UI.
- **End card**: transparent brand card overlaid on the last ~3.5s, not appended.

## VO recorded later
Deliver a picture edit with silent sections plus a timing sheet (`references/beat-sheet-template.md`), then fit VO, caption, mix.
