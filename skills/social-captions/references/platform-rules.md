# Platform rules

- **Instagram**: hook line first (visible before "more"), 2–4 short lines, CTA (Save/Share, link in bio), then 5–10 hashtags mixing broad (e.g. #AI), niche (e.g. #AIAgents), and a brand tag.
- **X**: under 280 characters; hook plus one insight; 1–2 hashtags max; no hashtag walls.
- **TikTok**: short, punchy, searchable phrase (keywords drive TikTok search); 3–5 hashtags; CTA to follow or save.
