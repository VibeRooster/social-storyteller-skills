# Social visuals

## Sizes
See `references/sizes.md`. Keep vertical-video text out of the top ~250px and bottom ~350px.

## Text on image
- One idea per graphic; ≤12 words of headline; emphasize one phrase only.
- Min ~30px body at 1200 wide, 50px+ for emphasis; check at phone size.
- High contrast; never text straight over busy imagery (use a panel or scrim).
- ≥60px safe margin on every side.
- Render text with real fonts in code (Pillow/HTML), never with an image generator.

## Brand
- Use the brand palette and type pair; ship two colorways so the author can pick.
- Personal/neutral posts: drop brand marks when asked; check before adding a logo.
- No official third-party logos or film stills in branded content.

## Carousels
Slide 1 = hook, readable in under 2s. One point per slide, same grid and type scale, progress cue, end on CTA/summary. All slides same size (1080x1350 preferred).

## Thumbnails and covers
Face or clear subject, 3–5 title words, one accent color. Pick the frame by hand. For a series, fix the template and script the refill.

## Video overlays
Transparent PNG, or ProRes 4444 / VP9 WebM with alpha. Premultiplied soft edges (no dark halos). Preview on flat color and a real frame.

## Pairing with HEIT posts
The image carries the hook or the single most quotable line; the post text carries the rest. Don't repeat the full post on the image.
